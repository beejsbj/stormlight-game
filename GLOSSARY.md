# Glossary

Terms for the Stormlight game workspace. Definitions marked (B) are Burooj's and are not yet agreed with Ashar.

**Demo** (B)
The final playable adaptation of one section of the books. The current pick is Kaladin's leap and Second Ideal in *The Way of Kings*, ch. 67 "Words".
_Avoid_: using "demo" or "demo scene" for an early exploratory build. Ashar's "demo scene" on the 2026-11-17 call `[34:59]` is a **Prototype** in these terms.

**Prototype** (B)
A quick, exploratory build on its own branch. It doesn't have to follow the books.

**Slice** (B)
One standalone fragment explored in concept: an emotional beat from a conversation, a gameplay moment, a Surge in battle, how a spren looks. Slices don't have to connect to each other. A slice may be an image, music, a 3D sketch, or a mechanic tried in a sandbox.

**Bible** (B)
The accumulated design reference in `docs/bible/`, built up from slices. It is the path to the **Demo**.
